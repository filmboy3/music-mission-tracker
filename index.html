Why I’m doing this

Music gave me a way through. This is how I give something back.

I’m Jonathan Schwartz—a singer-songwriter and former Broadway actor, raised around music and musicians. Music has always been a source of healing, connection, and purpose for me.

The planet offers that same nourishment every day. So it’s time to put some music in Mother Earth’s ears. This project plants seeds with listeners—turning a growing community into trees planted, clean water given, and ocean plastic removed: real, trackable change our planet desperately needs.

All streaming royalties are donated too, separately, through DistroKid.
