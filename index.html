Why I’m doing this

Music gave me a way through.
This is how I give something back.

I’m Jonathan Schwartz—a singer-songwriter and former Broadway actor, raised around music and musicians. Music has always been a source of healing, connection, and purpose for me.

The planet offers that same nourishment every day. At a moment when connection and action matter, this project brings the two together: sharing the music, growing a community, and turning that momentum into real, trackable environmental work. Streaming royalties support conservation, and every completed impact is recorded here.
