Why I’m doing this

Music gave me a way through.
This is how I give something back.

A debilitating injury put a guitar in my hands. I had to sit still for the first time in years, and I found I loved writing. 100+ songs later, I’m still following wherever the music takes me.

I spent nearly a decade on Broadway across three shows — and 50+ musicals before that, going back to childhood. Eventually I wanted to write and sing something that was mine, so I taught myself production from scratch.

Somewhere in there I started paying closer attention to the good happening in the world, and wanted my music to be part of it rather than separate from it. I’m putting my own money where my microphone is.

All streaming royalties are donated separately, through DistroKid. The mission goals above are funded personally.
